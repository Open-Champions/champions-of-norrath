## Authored but never executed

Everything. The sources compile for the EE, and their host contracts ran on the host with recording stubs; no unit has run on the EE or been compared with the original binary. Specifically unverified:

- The three large state machines (`asyncSaveGame__Fv`, `asyncLoadGame__Fv`, `asyncChangePlayers__Fv`) and `frontendRunControllerConfig__Fv` were transcribed from the instruction stream by hand; the host contracts check my reading, not the original.
- Delay-slot ordering was followed where it changes visible order (for example `MC_KickProtect` is cleared before `undoBrowser__Fv` in `asyncSaveGame__Fv`); other stores that sit in delay slots were kept in source order.
- The data objects (`MCA_Present`, `MCA_Formatted`, `MC_SpawnPoint`, `netLocalPad`, `padBlank`, `mcRunCmdNo`, `MCA_FileOpen`, `MC_FrontEndImport`) have only the sizes read from the symbol table. The executable keeps most of them in `.data` as zero words; the authored definitions have no initialiser, so they land in `.bss`. Section placement and load layout are not claimed.

## Deliberate differences from the original

- The `async*` services call `popPause__Fi(0)`/`pushPause__Fv()` where the original inlines their bodies (the inline body starts with the same `netNetworkMode` test).
- `asyncSaveGame__Fv` stores the CP0 Count register in a function-local `cycleStart` that nothing reads; the compiler removes the store, the `mfc0` stays.
- `frontendRunControllerConfig__Fv` reads label 14 from a two-halfword terminator pair that the source sets to an empty string; the original reads an uninitialised stack word there (an unknown button value moves the row counter to 14).
- Function-local statics whose extents the executable does not give are sized from the gap to the next symbol: `localSlotFileName` 672 bytes, `lname` 368 bytes.

## Unknown

- The meaning of words 4, 12 and 16 of the 88-byte `pad` and `netLocalPad` records beyond the bit tests used here (word 4 nonzero reads as a present controller; the bit values were taken from the code, not from a named constant, except `padAcceptButton` and `padBackButton`).
- What each syncState value means in `asyncSaveGame__Fv`, `asyncLoadGame__Fv` and `asyncChangePlayers__Fv` beyond what the code does; states 5 to 8 of `asyncSaveGame__Fv`, 6 to 8 of `asyncLoadGame__Fv` and 8 of `asyncChangePlayers__Fv` do nothing.
- The layout of the 23984-byte player record beyond bytes 0, 84, 89, 92, 172, 232 and 21872 (the last is a pointer to an object whose bytes 16 to 27 and 56 to 57 are copied).
- The layout of `ramSlot` (0xb29c8 bytes), `playerSelectInfo` (41-byte records with the name first and a byte at 32), `sortedDir` (64-byte records with the name at 32), `slotInfoHeaders` (112-byte records, a halfword at 106) and `MC_GameConfig` (button records of nine words at 52 + 36 * player).
- The queue-entry layout beyond four words; what `serviceResult__FP8queueCmdPi` does with the local copy.
- The numeric values and meanings of the `runConfigure__FiPUsi` and `runBrowser__FPUs` results beyond -2, -1, 0 and 1 as used.
- Whether the four halfwords at `playerChangeOldYaw` and the twelve floats at `playerChangeOldPos` are yaw and position, as the names suggest; the names come from the executable's symbol table.
